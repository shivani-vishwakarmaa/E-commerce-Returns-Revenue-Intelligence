# E-commerce Returns and Revenue Analysis

This project uses SQL to explore sales, customer activity, and cancelled or returned orders. The data is stored in four CSV files, and the analysis is in `analysis-queries.sql`.


## Dataset

| File | Rows | Contents |
|---|---:|---|
| `customers.csv` | 700 | Customer details; 121 VIP, 461 Regular, and 118 New customers |
| `products.csv` | 60 | Product details across five categories, with 12 products in each |
| `orders.csv` | 1,826 | Orders dated in 2025; 1,501 delivered, 158 cancelled, and 167 returned |
| `order_items.csv` | 4,613 | Products, quantities, purchase prices, and discounts for each order item |

## Files

- `customers.csv` contains customer details, including city and customer segment.
- `products.csv` contains product names, categories, costs, and prices.
- `orders.csv` contains order dates, statuses, shipping locations, and payment methods.
- `order_items.csv` contains the products and quantities in each order, along with purchase prices and discounts.
- `analysis-queries.sql` contains the eight queries used for this analysis.

## Questions explored

- What was the net revenue from delivered orders?
- Which product categories generated the most revenue?
- How was revenue split across customer segments?
- Which customers had the highest lifetime spend?
- What were the cancellation and return rates by product category?
- Which customer cities had the highest cancellation and return rates?
- How did margins in November and December compare with the rest of the year?
- How many customers made repeat purchases, and how many had no delivered orders?

## Results

- Fashion had the highest cancellation and return rate at 35.6%. Other categories ranged from 20% to 22%.
- Lucknow and Nagpur had the highest cancellation and return rates among the listed cities, at 32.1% and 30.3%.
- 121 VIP customers generated ₹2.41 crore in revenue, close to the ₹2.40 crore generated by 423 Regular customers.
- The reported margin was 35.4% for the rest of the year and 29.9% in November and December.
- The repeat-purchase rate was 58.1%; 67 customers had no delivered orders.
- Net revenue from delivered orders was ₹4.93 crore.
